# Northgate University - Library Access Rules

Northgate University is a fictional institution used only for demonstrating this software.

## Opening hours

The Deakin Library is open from 08:00 to 22:00 Monday to Friday, and from 10:00 to 18:00 on
Saturday. The library is closed on Sunday and on public holidays.

During the examination period the library extends weekday closing to 01:00.

## Borrowing

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 12 items at a time. Postgraduate students may borrow up to
25 items. Standard loans run for 21 days and renew automatically twice unless another reader has
requested the item.

Short-loan items are restricted to 4 hours and may not leave the building.

## Fines

Overdue standard loans accrue a fine of 20 pence per item per day, capped at 15 pounds per item.
Overdue short-loan items accrue 1 pound per hour. Borrowing is suspended once unpaid fines exceed
25 pounds.

## Visitors

Members of the public may read on site by arrangement with the Enquiries Desk but may not borrow.
