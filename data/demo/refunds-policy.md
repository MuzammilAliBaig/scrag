# Northgate University - Tuition Refund Policy

Northgate University is a fictional institution used only for demonstrating this software.

## Withdrawal before term begins

A student who withdraws in writing before the first day of term receives a full refund of tuition,
less a non-refundable administrative fee of 75 pounds.

## Withdrawal during term

Refunds during term are calculated on the calendar day of written notice:

- Withdrawal within the first two weeks: 80 per cent of tuition is refunded.
- Withdrawal between weeks three and six: 50 per cent of tuition is refunded.
- Withdrawal after week six: no tuition refund is issued.

The administrative fee of 75 pounds is retained in every case.

## Method and timing

Refunds are returned to the original method of payment. The Finance Office issues refunds within
30 working days of approving a withdrawal request. Refunds cannot be transferred to a third party.

## Appeals

A student may appeal a refund decision within 21 days of receiving it by writing to the Registrar.
Appeals submitted after 21 days are not considered.
