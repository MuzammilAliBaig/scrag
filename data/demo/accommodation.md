# Northgate University - Accommodation Handbook

Northgate University is a fictional institution used only for demonstrating this software.

## Eligibility

Guaranteed accommodation is offered to all first-year undergraduates who accept an offer by
31 July and name Northgate as their firm choice. Returning students enter a ballot held in March.

## Contracts

Standard residence contracts run for 40 weeks. A 51-week contract is available in Halloway Court
only. Contracts may not be terminated early except where the student withdraws from the University
or is granted a release by the Accommodation Office.

## Deposits

A deposit of 300 pounds is payable on acceptance and is returned within 45 days of the end of the
contract, less any deductions for damage or unpaid charges.

## Conduct

Quiet hours run from 23:00 to 07:00 every day. Candles, portable heaters and deep-fat fryers are
prohibited in all residences. Pets are not permitted, with the exception of registered assistance
animals.
