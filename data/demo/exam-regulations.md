# Northgate University - Examination Regulations

Northgate University is a fictional institution used only for demonstrating this software.

## Attendance

Candidates must arrive at the examination room at least 15 minutes before the start time.
A candidate arriving more than 30 minutes after the start may not be admitted, and no additional
time is granted to a candidate admitted late.

## Permitted materials

Only pens, pencils, and a transparent pencil case may be brought to the desk. Calculators are
permitted only where the paper's rubric states so explicitly. Mobile telephones and smart watches
must be switched off and placed in the bag at the front of the room.

## Illness and mitigating circumstances

A candidate unable to sit an examination through illness must submit a mitigating circumstances
form with supporting evidence within 5 working days of the examination date. The Board of
Examiners considers such forms at its next scheduled meeting.

## Results

Provisional results are released through the student portal no later than 6 weeks after the last
examination in the series. Results are not given by telephone.
